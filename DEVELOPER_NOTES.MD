# Human QA Engineer as the Reviewer
Unlike other domains where I had a cursory to expert level of knowledge in, this project has gone well outside of domains I normally work in.

I have experienced for the first time, "Comprehension Debt". SO, we are immediately introducing guards by isolating our prototype area from our production ready area.

I will allow Claude to own what it does in the prototype area and write whatever tests it needs to satisfy it's criteria. In the same way I allow a Junior developer to do the same.

But I need to take ownership of any test or line of code heading towards the production ready area so I can gain knowledge of the actual domain and implementation.

So while my AI flies at 5000 MPH, I will travel at 88 MPH to make sure we are headed in the right direction and realign as necessary.

"AI accelerates in the direction you point it, so the direction matters..."
