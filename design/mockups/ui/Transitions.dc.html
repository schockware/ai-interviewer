Transitions
1 · S · listening → thinking
The user's speech ended naturally. The server waits waitCadence after they go quiet.
2 · S · thinking → speech-ready
Response ready. cadence says when speech starts, so lead-in cues can fire.
3 · S · speech-ready → speaking
cadence elapsed. Carries transcript and audioStream.
4 · C · speaking → speech-ended
The last frame has played. guard, then transition, plus screenReaderPadding if set.
5 · S · speech-ended → listening
Guards release only after the server answers listening. Thinking shows until then (B).
6 · S · thinking, speech-ready or speaking → interrupted
The user speaks over the AI. The server works out where to re-enter.
7 · S · interrupted → listening
Shown as Listening. Captions and indicators are held meanwhile.
8 · C · any running state → pausing
The user presses pause, including in the opening and closing. Cancels speech or a guard in progress. Thinking shows. Not from not-started, session-closed, pausing, paused, resuming or timed-out.
9 · S · pausing → paused
The server confirms the pause.
10 · C · paused → resuming
The user presses resume.
11 · S · resuming → listening
The AI is back where it needs to be. Thinking shows until then. Exception: paused during the opening or closing, resuming re-enters preamble-started or postamble-started instead.
12 · C · not-started → preamble-started
The user presses Start Interview. Uninterruptible shows: the interviewer is not listening. Inside: notice, hold with a progress bar, then the voice.
13 · C · preamble-started → preamble-ended
The last frame of the opening has played. Same guard, transition and padding as row 4 (proposal).
14 · S · preamble-ended → thinking
The AI takes the first turn. Thinking shows.
15 · S · thinking → postamble-started
The AI concludes the interview. Same notice and hold, then the closing, which cannot be interrupted.
16 · C · postamble-started → postamble-ended
The last frame of the closing has played. Same guard as row 13.
17 · S · postamble-ended → session-closed
The server closes the session. Idle shows.
18 · C · waiting on the server → delayed
5 s without an answer (5 s elapsed). The client resends the waited-on cue. Warning: “The Interviewer isn't ready yet.”
19 · C · delayed → reconnecting
10 s more (15 s elapsed). The client resends the cue. Warning: “The Interviewer is struggling to reconnect.”
20 · C · reconnecting → timed-out
15 s more (30 s elapsed). Error: “The Interviewer has timed out. We are logging the error now. We will notify you when the session is ready to continue.”
21 · S · timed-out → paused
The server recovers and the user is notified. They press resume to continue.
22 · C · any state → session-closed
The user presses End interview. Goes straight to session-closed, skipping the closing. From any state except not-started and session-closed. Cancels speech or a guard in progress.
Decided

A Pause from any running state goes to pausing and shows Thinking.
B After speech-ended, Thinking shows until the server answers listening.
C Entrance: not-started, preamble-started, preamble-ended, thinking. Exit: thinking, postamble-started, postamble-ended, session-closed. not-started and session-closed show Idle. The four opening and closing states show Uninterruptible: an ear with a slash, meaning the interviewer is not listening.
D Pause during the speech-ended guard cancels it and goes to pausing. Guard, transition and padding stay as timed phases inside their states; a phase becomes a state only when its exits or shown cue differ. The opening and closing reuse the speech-ended guard.
E Waiting on the server: warning at 5 s, second warning 10 s later (15 s elapsed), error 15 s later (30 s elapsed). The client resends the waited-on cue at each warning.
F Resuming after a pause in the opening or closing re-enters that state, and repeats the notice and the hold once per entry.
G An End interview control takes any state straight to session-closed. It sits below the control group, apart from the other buttons, and asks for confirmation first. The confirmation copy is approved. Drawn on the Interview screen board.
H Uninterruptible notice, as a banner and one polite announcement: “The interviewer is not listening during this portion of the interview.”
I Text and braille readers would collide with the interviewer’s voice, so each opening and closing starts with a notice, then a hold with a progress bar, then the voice. The hold is a timed phase inside preamble-started and postamble-started, not a state.
J The hold length is a placeholder, [HOLD], until the timing is known. Everyone sees the hold and its progress bar.
K The hold has no skip control, because we do not yet know that we can control the stream.
L Uninterruptible is on the States board and in SPEC section 4.
M The names delayed, reconnecting and timed-out are accepted.

Criteria are my reading, to be confirmed in manual passes (SPEC section 12, item 4).

4.1.3 Warnings are status messages (role status, polite). The error is role alert. Focus never moves.
1.4.1, 1.1.1 Every message, and the icon, has a visible text label. The icon is decorative; colour is never the only signal.
1.4.3, 1.4.11 Text at least 4.5:1, icon and borders at least 3:1.
2.2.1 Warnings and the error stay until resolved. Nothing auto-dismisses.
1.4.10, 1.4.12 Readable at 320 px wide and with text spacing overridden.
2.4.11 A message never covers the focused control.
2.1.1, 2.5.8 Any retry, dismiss or End interview control works by keyboard and is at least 24 by 24 px.
Progress bar. It has a name and a fixed text value. Its movement is not announced.
Confirmation dialog. Focus moves in and returns to End interview; Escape closes it; Keep going is focused first (2.4.3, 2.1.2).
