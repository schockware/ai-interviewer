Status banner and preparation progress

Two shared components. The banner has a reserved slot on every page. The progress bar is on every setup page. Each page imports them, so they look and behave the same everywhere.

Rules

  • One banner at a time. If several apply, the most urgent wins: problem, then uploading, then waiting or retrying, then ready.
  • The banner is a status message (role status, polite). It is announced when it appears or changes, never moves focus, and keeps its text label as well as its icon and colour.
  • The progress bar has a name and a fixed text value. Its movement is not announced.
  • If preparation fails, nothing interrupts the user. The bar slows, the banner says so, and the diagnosis appears on the page.
  • The banner is not dismissible while it is true. It clears itself when the work is done.
