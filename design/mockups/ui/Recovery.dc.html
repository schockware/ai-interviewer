When the handoff wobbles

Pauses and lag must never look like a finished turn. Each case gets its own cue.

Timings: proposals

1 · Long silence before you answer

Your turn

Take your time. Would you like me to repeat the question?

After about 5–10 s of silence, the interviewer offers a soft prompt. It is spoken and also shown, and it never moves on to a new question.

2 · Interviewer cut in by mistake

Speaking Your turn

Sorry, go ahead.

The interviewer stopped because you kept talking.

With “interviewer yields”, the cue returns to Your turn with no chime. The session report counts it as a false cutoff by the interviewer, not a mistake by you.

3 · “Give me a sec”

Take your time holding 0:12

The interviewer will not respond until you start talking or press Ready.

This turns off end-of-turn detection. The control responds locally within 100 ms and is never delayed by the simulator.

4 · First time a preset is on

Simulated: Laggy / clipped

Cues will arrive late, like a real call

The interviewer’s voice and status lag on purpose. Your own buttons stay instant. Captions stay accurate unless you choose otherwise.

Shown once and dismissable. After that, the badge stays in the header for as long as the preset is on.
