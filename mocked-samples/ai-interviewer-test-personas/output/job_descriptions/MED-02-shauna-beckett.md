# Content Writer
Fernwood Financial Learning | Charlotte, NC

## About us
Fernwood Financial Learning publishes plain-language guides and courses on budgeting, saving and retirement. Its editorial team works closely with licensed advisors to review content.

## What you'll do
- Write accurate, accessible articles and guides on personal finance topics.
- Work with subject-matter experts to verify technical content.
- Plan content around audience questions and search trends.
- Maintain brand voice and update older content on a regular schedule.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years of professional writing experience.
- Ability to explain complex topics in plain language.
- Experience working with subject-matter reviewers.
- Knowledge of search-informed content planning.

Fernwood Financial Learning is an equal opportunity employer.
