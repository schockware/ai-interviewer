# Front-End Developer
Tallgrass Health Marketplace | Chicago, IL

## About us
Tallgrass Health Marketplace helps families compare and enroll in insurance plans through a consumer web application. The team is expanding its design system and testing practices.

## What you'll do
- Build and maintain user-facing features in React.
- Contribute to the design system and shared components.
- Write unit and end-to-end tests for new interfaces.
- Partner with designers and back-end engineers on API integration.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years of front-end development experience.
- Proficiency in React and TypeScript.
- Working knowledge of accessibility standards.
- Experience with automated front-end testing.

Tallgrass Health Marketplace is an equal opportunity employer.
