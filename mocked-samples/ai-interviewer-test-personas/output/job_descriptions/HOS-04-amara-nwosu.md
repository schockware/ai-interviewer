# Travel Consultant
Horizon Ledger Travel Partners | Atlanta, GA

## About us
Horizon Ledger Travel Partners is a mid-sized travel management firm serving both corporate accounts and high-end leisure clients. It is building a premium advisory team and a structured training program for new hires.

## What you'll do
- Lead a team of 6 travel advisors and set weekly priorities and goals
- Own relationships with top 25 corporate and leisure accounts
- Monitor team production, margin and client satisfaction metrics
- Develop and deliver advisor training on products, systems and service standards

## What we're looking for
- 8 or more years of travel industry experience
- Prior experience mentoring or supervising advisors
- Strong knowledge of corporate travel programs and leisure products
- Comfort with sales reporting and performance metrics

Horizon Ledger Travel Partners is an equal opportunity employer.
