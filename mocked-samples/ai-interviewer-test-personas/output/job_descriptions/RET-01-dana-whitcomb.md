# Store Associate (grocery)
Mesquite Row Grocery | Tucson, AZ

## About us
Mesquite Row Grocery is a family-owned neighborhood supermarket with three stores in the Tucson area. The company focuses on local produce, a full-service deli and a loyal community customer base.

## What you'll do
- Receive, stock and face produce and refrigerated products to company display standards
- Greet customers, answer product questions and help locate items
- Monitor product freshness and rotate stock using date codes
- Assist with weekly ad price changes and shelf signage

## What we're looking for
- Previous grocery or retail experience preferred
- Ability to lift up to 40 pounds and stand for full shifts
- Weekend and early-morning availability
- Food handler card or willingness to obtain one

Mesquite Row Grocery is an equal opportunity employer.
