# Compliance Analyst
Granite Peak Financial | New York, NY

## About us
Granite Peak Financial is a regional bank holding company with growing digital deposit and payments businesses. The compliance department is building out a second-line oversight function.

## What you'll do
- Oversee compliance monitoring and testing for the digital banking and payments business.
- Review new products and partnerships for regulatory risk before launch.
- Manage and mentor a team of three analysts.
- Report compliance metrics and issues to the Chief Compliance Officer and board committee.

## What we're looking for
- 10+ years in bank compliance, including AML.
- Experience with fintech or payments partnerships is a plus.
- Team leadership or mentoring experience.
- Strong communicator able to brief senior leadership.

Granite Peak Financial is an equal opportunity employer.
