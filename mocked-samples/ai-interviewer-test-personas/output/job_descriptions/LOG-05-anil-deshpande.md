# Supply Chain Analyst
Atlas Meridian Supply Solutions | Atlanta, GA

## About us
Atlas Meridian Supply Solutions manages distribution and procurement programs for healthcare and industrial customers. The analytics group supports network design, inventory strategy, and transportation cost reduction.

## What you'll do
- Analyze transportation spend and recommend mode and carrier changes
- Model inventory policies including safety stock and reorder points
- Build and maintain business intelligence dashboards for network performance
- Present findings and recommendations to operations and customer stakeholders

## What we're looking for
- Bachelor's degree in supply chain, engineering, or analytics
- 3 or more years of supply chain analysis experience
- Strong SQL and Excel skills; experience with a BI tool
- Exposure to transportation or network optimization is a plus

Atlas Meridian Supply Solutions is an equal opportunity employer.
