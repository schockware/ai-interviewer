# Front Desk Agent (hotel)
The Gilded Anchor Hotel | Savannah, GA

## About us
The Gilded Anchor Hotel is a 140-room boutique property in a historic district, known for personalized service. It is part of a small independent hotel group.

## What you'll do
- Welcome guests, manage arrivals and departures and maintain accurate account records
- Handle phone and email reservations, including special requests and package add-ons
- Work with concierge and housekeeping to resolve guest needs quickly
- Complete shift reports and cash drawer reconciliation during overnight and evening shifts

## What we're looking for
- Front desk or guest service experience in a hotel or similar setting
- Ability to work evenings, overnights, weekends and holidays
- Strong attention to detail with cash handling and billing
- Professional appearance and communication

The Gilded Anchor Hotel is an equal opportunity employer.
