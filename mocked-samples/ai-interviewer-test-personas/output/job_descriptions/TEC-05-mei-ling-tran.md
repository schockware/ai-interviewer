# IT Help Desk Technician
Alder Point Insurance Group | Sacramento, CA

## About us
Alder Point Insurance Group is a regional insurer with about 600 employees across four offices. The IT service desk supports staff in claims, underwriting and sales.

## What you'll do
- Provide first and second-level support through phone, chat and ticketing.
- Manage user accounts, access requests and device provisioning.
- Document solutions and maintain the internal knowledge base.
- Escalate complex issues to network and systems teams.

## What we're looking for
- 1+ years of help desk or IT support experience.
- Familiarity with directory-based account management.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- Willingness to work a rotating schedule that includes occasional early shifts.

Alder Point Insurance Group is an equal opportunity employer.
