# Patient Access Representative
Cedar Ridge Regional Health | Madison, WI

## About us
Cedar Ridge Regional Health is a community health system operating a 180-bed hospital and several outpatient clinics. The Patient Access team is the first point of contact for scheduled and emergency patients.

## What you'll do
- Register and pre-register patients for hospital outpatient and emergency visits
- Verify insurance benefits, obtain authorizations, and estimate patient financial responsibility
- Explain consent forms and financial policies to patients and families
- Collect point-of-service payments and reconcile daily deposits

## What we're looking for
- High school diploma or equivalent; associate degree preferred
- 1 year of registration or customer service experience in a healthcare setting
- Working knowledge of insurance plan types and prior authorization
- Ability to work rotating shifts including some weekends

Cedar Ridge Regional Health is an equal opportunity employer.
