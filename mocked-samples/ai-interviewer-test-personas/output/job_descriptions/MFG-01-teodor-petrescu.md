# CNC Machine Operator
Ironwood Aerospace Fabrication | Columbus, OH

## About us
Ironwood Aerospace Fabrication builds machined structural components for regional aircraft programs. The Columbus plant runs three shifts and is expanding its five-axis capacity.

## What you'll do
- Set up and run 3-axis and 5-axis CNC machines to produce aerospace-grade titanium and aluminum parts.
- Interpret engineering drawings and apply GD&T when inspecting parts.
- Prove out new programs with the manufacturing engineer and record edits.
- Maintain traceability documentation for every lot.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years of CNC machining experience, including setup.
- Working knowledge of GD&T and precision inspection tools.
- Ability to edit and optimize CNC programs.
- Experience in a regulated or documented quality environment is preferred.

Ironwood Aerospace Fabrication is an equal opportunity employer.
