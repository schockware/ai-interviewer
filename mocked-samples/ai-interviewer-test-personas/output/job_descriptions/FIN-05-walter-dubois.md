# Insurance Underwriter
Ridgeway Specialty Underwriters | Hartford, CT

## About us
Ridgeway Specialty Underwriters writes complex commercial risks through a network of wholesale brokers. The company is launching a new middle-market property and casualty unit.

## What you'll do
- Underwrite middle-market property accounts with larger limits and layered programs.
- Develop pricing strategy and underwriting guidelines for the new unit.
- Build relationships with wholesale brokers and attend marketing events.
- Collaborate with reinsurance and actuarial teams on capacity and rate adequacy.

## What we're looking for
- 12+ years of commercial underwriting experience.
- Experience with larger-limit or catastrophe-exposed property is preferred.
- Demonstrated profitable portfolio management.
- Ability to coach and develop underwriters.

Ridgeway Specialty Underwriters is an equal opportunity employer.
