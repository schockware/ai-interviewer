# Fleet Scheduler
Riverbend Municipal Services Cooperative | Cincinnati, OH

## About us
Riverbend Municipal Services Cooperative provides shared waste collection and street maintenance equipment for several neighboring towns. The fleet team plans crews, trucks, and maintenance across more than 120 vehicles.

## What you'll do
- Schedule crews and vehicles for daily and seasonal service routes
- Coordinate preventive maintenance and repair downtime with the garage
- Monitor telematics data and recommend route changes
- Track labor hours and prepare weekly utilization and cost reports

## What we're looking for
- 4 or more years of fleet, dispatch, or transportation scheduling experience
- Strong spreadsheet and reporting skills
- Familiarity with union or collective-bargaining shift rules preferred
- Ability to manage competing priorities and urgent changes

Riverbend Municipal Services Cooperative is an equal opportunity employer.
