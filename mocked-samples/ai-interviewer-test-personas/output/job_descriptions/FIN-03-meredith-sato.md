# Financial Analyst
Harbor Point Insurance Services | Minneapolis, MN

## About us
Harbor Point Insurance Services is a managed-care and claims administration company. The finance team supports multiple lines of business with planning and performance reporting.

## What you'll do
- Prepare annual budgets and rolling forecasts for two business lines.
- Analyze claims and premium trends and explain variances to business leaders.
- Build and maintain financial models to evaluate new product pricing.
- Partner with actuarial and accounting on reserve and accrual inputs.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years in FP&A or corporate finance.
- Advanced modeling skills and strong written communication.
- Exposure to insurance or healthcare financials is preferred.
- Bachelor's degree in finance or accounting.

Harbor Point Insurance Services is an equal opportunity employer.
