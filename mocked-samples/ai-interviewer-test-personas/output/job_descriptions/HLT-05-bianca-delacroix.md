# Registered Nurse, Medical-Surgical
Seagrass Medical Center | Sarasota, FL

## About us
Seagrass Medical Center is a 250-bed hospital pursuing Magnet-style nursing excellence. The progressive care unit provides step-down care for patients transitioning out of the ICU or needing close monitoring.

## What you'll do
- Provide care for 3-4 progressive care patients with continuous cardiac monitoring
- Titrate approved medications per protocol and respond to changes in condition
- Educate patients and families on treatment plans and self-care at discharge
- Participate in unit-based councils and quality projects

## What we're looking for
- Active Florida RN license
- 2 or more years of acute care nursing experience
- BLS and ACLS required; PCCN preferred or obtained within 18 months
- Ability to interpret basic cardiac rhythms

Seagrass Medical Center is an equal opportunity employer.
