# Software Engineer
Meridian Fieldworks | Dallas, TX

## About us
Meridian Fieldworks builds scheduling and dispatch software for utility and field-service companies. Its engineering teams ship weekly and work closely with customer support.

## What you'll do
- Design and build services for the scheduling platform.
- Own features from planning through production monitoring.
- Review code and mentor newer engineers.
- Work with product managers to refine requirements.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years of professional back-end development experience.
- Strong SQL and a general-purpose language such as Python or Java.
- Experience designing and consuming APIs.
- Exposure to cloud deployment and monitoring.

Meridian Fieldworks is an equal opportunity employer.
