# Health Information Technician
Summit Pines Medical Center | Hartford, CT

## About us
Summit Pines Medical Center is a nonprofit teaching hospital serving central Connecticut. Its Health Information Management department supports coding, compliance, and data integrity for more than 60,000 annual encounters.

## What you'll do
- Code inpatient and outpatient records using ICD-10-CM/PCS and CPT
- Perform data integrity reviews and resolve duplicate or overlaid patient records
- Respond to coding queries and coordinate physician clarifications
- Contribute to monthly quality reporting and internal coding audits

## What we're looking for
- Associate degree in Health Information Technology or equivalent
- RHIT or CCS credential
- 3 or more years of hospital coding or HIM experience
- Experience with encoder software and Joint Commission documentation standards

Summit Pines Medical Center is an equal opportunity employer.
