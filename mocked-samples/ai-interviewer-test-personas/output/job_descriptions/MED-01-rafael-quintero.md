# Podcast Producer / Audio Editor
Lantern Story Network | Atlanta, GA

## About us
Lantern Story Network is an independent audio company producing documentary and conversation podcasts. It is growing its team to launch six new series next year.

## What you'll do
- Lead production of one or two series from planning through release.
- Edit and mix episodes to network quality standards.
- Manage freelance editors, hosts and guest schedules.
- Use listener data to inform format and release decisions.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years of podcast or audio production experience.
- Expert-level audio editing and mixing skills.
- Experience managing timelines and freelancers.
- Comfort working with listener analytics and sponsor deliverables.

Lantern Story Network is an equal opportunity employer.
