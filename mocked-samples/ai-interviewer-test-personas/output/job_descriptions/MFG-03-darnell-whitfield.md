# ERP Data Analyst (manufacturing)
Tidewater Industrial Controls | Charleston, SC

## About us
Tidewater Industrial Controls manufactures panels and sensors for water treatment plants. A growing business systems group supports finance, planning, and operations on a single ERP platform.

## What you'll do
- Own data quality standards for items, BOMs, and vendors across the ERP system.
- Build dashboards for inventory, production variance, and purchasing performance.
- Support the finance team with standard cost updates and month-end data pulls.
- Train plant users on data entry procedures.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years of experience with manufacturing ERP data.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with a BI or dashboard tool.
- Understanding of cost accounting concepts.
- Clear communicator with plant and finance staff.

Tidewater Industrial Controls is an equal opportunity employer.
