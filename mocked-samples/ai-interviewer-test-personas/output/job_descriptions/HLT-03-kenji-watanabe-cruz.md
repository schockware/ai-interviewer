# Medical Laboratory Technician
Redwood Crossing Health Network | Oakland, CA

## About us
Redwood Crossing Health Network runs a core laboratory that supports four hospitals and a dozen clinics in the East Bay. The lab operates around the clock with a focus on rapid turnaround and quality.

## What you'll do
- Perform testing in chemistry, hematology, coagulation, and basic microbiology
- Maintain instruments, run QC, and document corrective actions
- Cross-train in blood bank set-up and support the point-of-care testing program
- Participate in competency assessments and proficiency testing

## What we're looking for
- Associate degree in medical laboratory technology or related field
- Active California clinical laboratory technician or scientist licensure
- 3 or more years of clinical laboratory experience
- Willingness to rotate through evening and weekend shifts

Redwood Crossing Health Network is an equal opportunity employer.
