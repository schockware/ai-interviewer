# Warehouse Associate (forklift certified)
Canyon Rail Fulfillment | El Paso, TX

## About us
Canyon Rail Fulfillment operates a 400,000 sq ft e-commerce fulfillment center serving online retailers. The site runs two shifts with peak volume from October through January.

## What you'll do
- Operate powered industrial trucks to move pallets between receiving, storage, and shipping
- Pick, pack, and stage orders to meet hourly productivity targets
- Perform cycle counts and resolve inventory discrepancies
- Follow safety procedures and participate in daily team huddles

## What we're looking for
- Current forklift operator certification
- 6 months or more of warehouse experience
- Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and stand for a full shift
- Availability for overtime during peak season

Canyon Rail Fulfillment is an equal opportunity employer.
