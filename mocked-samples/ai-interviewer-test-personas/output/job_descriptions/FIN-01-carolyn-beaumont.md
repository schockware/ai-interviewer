# Bank Teller
Cedar Bluff Credit Union | Lincoln, NE

## About us
Cedar Bluff Credit Union is a member-owned institution serving about 40,000 members across eastern Nebraska. The credit union emphasizes personal service and member education.

## What you'll do
- Serve as a universal banker handling teller transactions and opening new accounts.
- Explain share, checking, and certificate products to members and recommend suitable options.
- Verify identity and follow BSA and anti-fraud procedures.
- Meet monthly member referral and service goals.

## What we're looking for
- 1+ year of cash handling or banking experience.
- Strong communication and sales aptitude.
- Willingness to learn lending and account opening systems.
- High school diploma required; associate degree preferred.

Cedar Bluff Credit Union is an equal opportunity employer.
