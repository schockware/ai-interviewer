# Clinical Documentation Specialist
Ironwood Health Partners | Cleveland, OH

## About us
Ironwood Health Partners is a multi-hospital system building a centralized clinical documentation integrity program. The team supports revenue integrity, quality reporting, and physician engagement across eight facilities.

## What you'll do
- Lead a team of CDI specialists and set daily review and query productivity targets
- Develop dashboards tracking query rates, response rates, and case mix trends
- Deliver physician and advanced practice provider education across facilities
- Collaborate with coding, compliance, and finance on denial prevention

## What we're looking for
- RN license or coding credential with CDI certification
- 8 or more years of healthcare experience including 4 years in CDI
- Prior experience leading or mentoring clinical staff
- Proficiency with reporting tools and CDI software

Ironwood Health Partners is an equal opportunity employer.
