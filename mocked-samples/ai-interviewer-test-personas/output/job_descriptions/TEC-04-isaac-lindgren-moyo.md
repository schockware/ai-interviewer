# Data Analyst
Kestrel Outdoor Supply | Denver, CO

## About us
Kestrel Outdoor Supply is an online and in-store retailer of camping and trail equipment. The analytics team supports merchandising, marketing and supply chain decisions.

## What you'll do
- Analyze sales, inventory and customer behavior to guide merchandising decisions.
- Design and maintain dashboards for category managers.
- Run and interpret marketing experiments.
- Present findings and recommendations to senior leaders.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years of analytics experience.
- Advanced SQL and experience with a scripting language.
- Experience with experimentation or A/B testing.
- Retail or e-commerce background preferred.

Kestrel Outdoor Supply is an equal opportunity employer.
