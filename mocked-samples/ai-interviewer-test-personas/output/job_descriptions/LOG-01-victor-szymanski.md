# Freight Dispatcher
Tri-State Distribution Network | Gary, IN

## About us
Tri-State Distribution Network is a regional asset-based carrier with a mix of company drivers and owner-operators. The operations team manages dedicated routes for retail and manufacturing customers.

## What you'll do
- Lead a dispatch pod of 50 trucks including company drivers and owner-operators
- Monitor service metrics and coach drivers on on-time performance
- Coordinate with customer service on appointment changes and exceptions
- Support the planning team in building weekly lane and capacity forecasts

## What we're looking for
- 5 or more years of dispatch or fleet operations experience
- Working knowledge of FMCSA hours-of-service rules
- Experience with TMS and telematics platforms
- Ability to supervise and mentor junior dispatchers

Tri-State Distribution Network is an equal opportunity employer.
