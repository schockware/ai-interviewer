# Reservations Agent (travel/airline)
Meridian Group Travel | Charlotte, NC

## About us
Meridian Group Travel arranges group airfare and lodging for sports teams, school trips and corporate travel. The reservations team supports group leaders from first quote through final ticketing.

## What you'll do
- Build and price group itineraries including air, hotel and ground transport
- Manage deposits, name lists and ticketing deadlines for groups of 10 to 200
- Serve as the main contact for group leaders before and during travel
- Coordinate with airline group desks and hotel sales offices

## What we're looking for
- 2 or more years of reservations or travel booking experience
- Working knowledge of at least one global distribution system or airline booking tool
- Strong follow-up and organizational skills
- Ability to manage multiple groups at different stages

Meridian Group Travel is an equal opportunity employer.
