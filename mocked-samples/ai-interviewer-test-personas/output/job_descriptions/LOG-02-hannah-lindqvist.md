# Logistics Coordinator
Harborgate Global Logistics | Chicago, IL

## About us
Harborgate Global Logistics is a third-party logistics provider handling ocean, air, and domestic freight for mid-market importers. Its account teams own day-to-day execution for named customers.

## What you'll do
- Manage ocean import shipments from booking through final-mile delivery
- Prepare and review shipping documents including bills of lading and commercial invoices
- Serve as the daily contact for assigned customer accounts
- Report on shipment milestones and flag delays proactively

## What we're looking for
- 3 or more years of logistics or freight forwarding experience
- Familiarity with import documentation and customs processes
- Strong written communication and attention to detail
- Proficiency with Excel and a TMS or freight forwarding system

Harborgate Global Logistics is an equal opportunity employer.
