# Sales Associate, Consumer Electronics
Circuitry Lane | San Antonio, TX

## About us
Circuitry Lane is a regional electronics and mobile retailer with 9 stores in Texas. It is known for knowledgeable staff and a service desk that helps customers get new devices running.

## What you'll do
- Guide customers through smartphone and carrier plan options and complete activations
- Meet monthly goals for accessories, protection plans and service add-ons
- Perform basic device setup and data transfers at the service counter
- Maintain accurate inventory counts for high-value merchandise

## What we're looking for
- Some retail sales experience, preferably in electronics
- Strong verbal communication and willingness to learn product details quickly
- Ability to follow security procedures for high-value inventory
- Weekend and holiday availability

Circuitry Lane is an equal opportunity employer.
