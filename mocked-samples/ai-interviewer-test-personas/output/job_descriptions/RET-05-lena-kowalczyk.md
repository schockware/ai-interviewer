# E-commerce Merchandising Coordinator
Birchwell Marketplace | Chicago, IL

## About us
Birchwell Marketplace is an online retailer of household and seasonal products with a growing private-label line. Its merchandising team of 12 sets assortment, pricing and on-site presentation.

## What you'll do
- Own assortment planning and on-site placement for the kitchen and dining category
- Analyze weekly sales, margin and inventory data and recommend markdowns or reorders
- Work with vendors on new product launches and promotional pricing
- Partner with marketing and creative on seasonal campaigns

## What we're looking for
- 3 or more years of e-commerce or merchandising experience
- Strong analytical skills and advanced spreadsheet proficiency
- Experience working with vendors or buyers preferred
- Ability to manage multiple deadlines across teams

Birchwell Marketplace is an equal opportunity employer.
