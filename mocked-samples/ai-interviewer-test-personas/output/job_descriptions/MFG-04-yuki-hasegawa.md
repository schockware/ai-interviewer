# Procurement Specialist
Summit Ridge Medical Devices | Hillsboro, OR

## About us
Summit Ridge Medical Devices designs and builds patient monitoring equipment. The supply chain team is adding a strategic sourcing role to reduce single-source risk.

## What you'll do
- Develop sourcing strategies for electronic and plastic components.
- Qualify and onboard new suppliers with quality and engineering.
- Lead quarterly business reviews with top vendors.
- Model total cost of ownership for make-versus-buy and dual-source decisions.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years in procurement or strategic sourcing, preferably electronics.
- Experience negotiating multi-year agreements.
- Familiarity with supplier quality requirements in a regulated industry.
- Strong analytical skills.

Summit Ridge Medical Devices is an equal opportunity employer.
