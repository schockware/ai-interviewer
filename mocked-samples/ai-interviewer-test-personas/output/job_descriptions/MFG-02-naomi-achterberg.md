# Production Planner
Northgate Appliance Components | Kalamazoo, MI

## About us
Northgate Appliance Components supplies motors and control assemblies to home appliance makers. The company operates two Michigan plants and is centralizing its planning function.

## What you'll do
- Manage the master production schedule across two plants and set planning policy.
- Lead and develop a team of five planners and schedulers.
- Own the monthly S&OP supply review and present constraints to leadership.
- Drive planning parameter clean-up in the ERP system.

## What we're looking for
- 10+ years in production planning, including 3+ years leading people.
- Strong knowledge of MRP logic and master scheduling.
- Experience with electronics or motor assembly is a plus.
- Ability to present supply plans to executives.

Northgate Appliance Components is an equal opportunity employer.
