# Assistant Store Manager
Oakmere and Co. Home | Cherry Hill, NJ

## About us
Oakmere and Co. Home operates 14 mid-priced home furnishings stores across the Mid-Atlantic. The company is expanding and promotes from within to staff new locations.

## What you'll do
- Manage all store operations, including sales, staffing, inventory and visual presentation
- Recruit, hire, train and develop a team of 25 to 30 associates and 3 supervisors
- Own the store P&L and prepare monthly performance reviews with the district manager
- Maintain loss prevention and compliance standards

## What we're looking for
- 5 or more years of retail experience with at least 2 in a management role
- Demonstrated ability to meet sales and labor targets
- Experience hiring and developing supervisors
- Comfort with P&L reports and budgeting

Oakmere and Co. Home is an equal opportunity employer.
