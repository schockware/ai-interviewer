# Linux Systems Administrator
Brightwater Cloud Services | Seattle, WA

## About us
Brightwater Cloud Services provides managed infrastructure to mid-sized healthcare and education customers. The platform team runs a hybrid environment of private servers and public cloud workloads.

## What you'll do
- Operate and improve a hybrid fleet of Linux servers and cloud instances.
- Build infrastructure-as-code templates and configuration management workflows.
- Participate in on-call rotation and lead post-incident reviews.
- Partner with security on hardening standards and audits.

## What we're looking for
- 8+ years of Linux systems administration experience.
- Experience with scripting and automation in Bash or Python.
- Hands-on exposure to public cloud platforms and infrastructure as code.
- Familiarity with container orchestration is a plus.

Brightwater Cloud Services is an equal opportunity employer.
