# Loan Processor
Brookhaven Home Lending | Charlotte, NC

## About us
Brookhaven Home Lending is a regional retail mortgage lender focused on first-time buyers and refinance customers. The company is growing its commercial-adjacent small loan division.

## What you'll do
- Process conventional and government-backed mortgage files with minimal rework.
- Review and disclose loan documents in line with compliance timelines.
- Act as the primary contact for loan officers, underwriters, and closing agents.
- Support quality control audits and resolve findings.

## What we're looking for
- 3+ years of mortgage processing experience.
- Working knowledge of agency and government loan guidelines.
- Strong organizational skills in a high-volume pipeline.
- Experience with compliance disclosure timelines.

Brookhaven Home Lending is an equal opportunity employer.
