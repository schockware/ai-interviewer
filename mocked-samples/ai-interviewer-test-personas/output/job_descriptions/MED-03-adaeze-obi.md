# Copy Editor
Harbor & Vale Publishing | Philadelphia, PA

## About us
Harbor & Vale Publishing is an independent house with a list of narrative nonfiction, essays and regional history. It publishes about 60 print and digital titles each year.

## What you'll do
- Copyedit manuscripts for grammar, consistency, style and factual accuracy.
- Maintain and update the house style sheet.
- Coordinate with authors, managing editors and freelance proofreaders.
- Review final pages and digital files before release.

## What we're looking for
- 8+ years of professional copyediting experience.
- Deep knowledge of book publishing style conventions.
- Experience managing freelancers and production schedules.
- Meticulous attention to detail and strong judgment on queries.

Harbor & Vale Publishing is an equal opportunity employer.
