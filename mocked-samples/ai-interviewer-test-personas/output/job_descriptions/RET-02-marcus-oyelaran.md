# Customer Service Representative (online retail)
Northgate Home Goods Online | Columbus, OH

## About us
Northgate Home Goods Online is a mid-sized e-commerce retailer selling furniture, decor and kitchen products to customers nationwide. Its customer experience team of 40 supports chat, email and social channels seven days a week.

## What you'll do
- Lead a pod of 8 customer service representatives and manage daily queue coverage
- Review quality scores, give weekly feedback and run team huddles
- Handle escalated customer cases and approve exceptions within policy
- Report on contact volume, response time and customer satisfaction trends

## What we're looking for
- 3 or more years of customer service experience in e-commerce
- Prior experience coaching or mentoring teammates
- Familiarity with ticketing platforms and reporting dashboards
- Availability to work a rotating weekend schedule

Northgate Home Goods Online is an equal opportunity employer.
