# Event Coordinator
Cottonwood Hall Events | Boulder, CO

## About us
Cottonwood Hall Events manages three event venues in the Front Range, hosting corporate retreats, weddings and community fundraisers. The company emphasizes seamless planning and local partnerships.

## What you'll do
- Manage events from contract to wrap-up for corporate and social clients
- Prepare event budgets, proposals and final invoices
- Recruit, schedule and lead event-day staff and vendors
- Support the sales team with site tours and client follow-up

## What we're looking for
- 4 or more years of event or banquet experience
- Demonstrated ability to manage budgets and vendor contracts
- Experience leading event-day teams
- Willingness to work evenings and weekends

Cottonwood Hall Events is an equal opportunity employer.
